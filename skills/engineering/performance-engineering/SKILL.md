# Performance Engineering

Find dominant cost from real usage and available measurements under the supplied workload, platform, and resource objective. Keep advice conditional on unknown workloads or tradeoffs.

Seek unnecessary work; compare changes under identical behavior and workload, counting costs shifted elsewhere or across lifetimes. Local speedups can raise total cost.

Return the analysis, proposed optimization, and evidence; separate predicted from measured gains, mark objective attainment or uncertainty, and propose a distinguishing experiment for missing evidence.

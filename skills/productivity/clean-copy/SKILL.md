# Clean Copy

Throughout the artifact, keep reader-facing content and remove production-only material according to the supplied audience, purpose, structure, and voice. Instructions may themselves be reader-facing.

Return the cleaned artifact. When audience or purpose leaves inclusion uncertain, preserve that uncertainty outside the artifact rather than silently discard potentially intended content.

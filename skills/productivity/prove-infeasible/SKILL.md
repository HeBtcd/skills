# Prove Infeasible

Derive incompatible conditions from a candidate and established constraints; give premises, sources, and inference. Exclude only covered scope; an abstraction excludes a candidate only if the candidate necessarily satisfies it.

Distinguish inconsistent premises from conflict with consistent ones. A failed implementation does not exclude its design class; no solution found is not proof. Keep unsupported premises conditional.

Return the exact derivation/scope or missing fact/step; reconsider exclusions when premises change. Failure to prove impossibility does not prove feasibility.

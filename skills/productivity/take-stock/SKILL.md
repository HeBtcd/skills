# Take Stock

Reconstruct the requested scope from sourced decisions, actual results, and unresolved dependencies. Separate proposals/completion reports from evidence of completion. Preserve qualifications and corrections; old conclusions cannot override newer decisions.

Resolve obtainable factual gaps. Return settled decisions, evidenced results, and remaining questions, including dependencies governing next steps. Progress grants no new permission.

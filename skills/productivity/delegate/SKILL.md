# Delegate

Delegate independently completable, reviewable work only when savings exceed coordination cost.

- worker: implements an established design.
- critic: evaluates against acceptance criteria.
- independent: derives without the incumbent candidate.

Assign one integrator, retrievable requirements, change boundaries, and sufficient discretion. Keep one writer per mutable artifact and authority acyclic. Investigate open questions before implementing dependent choices.

Independent derivation excludes the incumbent and prior reasoning from inherited context and workspace baseline; otherwise report the mismatch and propose a suitable role.

Transfer responsibility only after confirmed child creation and a reliable return route. Native delivery guarantees suffice; seek acknowledgement only for unresolved delivery. Launch/delivery failure leaves responsibility with the integrator. Provide a supported blocker/decision route.

Continue independent work or wait on dependencies. Integrate results with evidence and unresolved dependencies; launch is not completion.

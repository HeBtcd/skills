# Now What

Compare acting, waiting, and learning against the objective. Unless a choice is mandated, favor reducing consequential uncertainty with little irreversible loss, including delay cost.

Use decision-relevant partial evidence with its limits. Experiments cannot settle user preferences; routes requiring changed requirements remain conditional.

Return the next action, why now, and evidence or owner choice that would reverse it.

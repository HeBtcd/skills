# Hands-On

A stronger manager owns design, integration, and verification. Give a weaker, capable implementer settled design, retrievable requirements, a bounded change, permitted local choices, and verification criteria.

The implementer returns the change, evidence, and gaps; decisions beyond the brief stay with the manager. The manager checks conformity and integrates or identifies corrections.

Return results and unresolved gaps. The split is unsuitable if design cannot bound implementation or coordination costs exceed savings.

# Minimal Model

Start from required results and uses; identify what to minimize. Fix requirements within comparisons, update the model when they change, and leave choices dependent on missing intent unresolved.

Construct a complete candidate without inheriting existing parts. Justify each retained part by a requirement or constraint that fails without it, allowing derivation or combination. Preserve required information and relationships in a form suited to use.

Compare whole alternatives and removals, counting hidden or shifted work. Brevity alone proves neither completeness nor necessity.

Return the model, completeness evidence, retention reasons, and unresolved reductions. Bound minimality claims by the alternatives and measure considered.

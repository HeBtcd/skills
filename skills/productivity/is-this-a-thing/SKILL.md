# Is This a Thing?

Describe the mechanism without local names; search plausible fields for concepts matching its structure and behavior. Check meanings, not shared names or loose analogies.

Choose the narrowest contradiction-free explanation of relevant behavior. Distinguish core-operation from whole-workflow matches; lacking one term does not justify splitting the capability.

Return the supported match and sources, or closest candidates, mismatches, and search limits. Expose missing behavioral information.

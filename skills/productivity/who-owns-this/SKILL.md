# Who Owns This?

Find who has the information and authority to define a rule and control to maintain it. Execution does not confer policy authority; guarantees cannot exceed knowledge or control.

Separate roles only when consequential. Return ownership and its basis, distinguishing existing from proposed assignments and exposing missing knowledge or authority.

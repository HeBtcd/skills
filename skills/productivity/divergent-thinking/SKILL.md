# Divergent Thinking

Vary unsettled assumptions to generate materially different outcomes; preserve confirmed requirements unless reconsideration is requested. Current implementations and examples do not bound the search.

Count alternatives only when observations distinguish their results. Return them with changed assumptions and missing information that would distinguish or eliminate them. Keep possibilities conditional and selection open.

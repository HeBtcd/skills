# Check Premises

Check result-changing premises, including the model's, starting with the most consequential uncertainty. Does evidence support their exact meaning, scope, and current use, and does the inference follow?

Keep unsupported premises and dependent conclusions conditional, not false; reconsider conclusions when their basis changes. Check internally unless a premise changes or blocks the answer, then expose the dependency and evidence needed.

# What I Said

Confirm requirements only from user words or explicit confirmation supporting that proposition and scope. Attach a quote or retrievable source; preserve qualifications. Authorization does not confirm unstated requirements.

Apply corrections only within superseded scope; retain unaffected confirmations and unresolved conflicts or ambiguous assent.

Return sourced confirmations separately from unresolved interpretations. Label inferences and delegated choices; neither is user confirmation.

# Go With the Grain

Inspect an established example; map relevant parts and relationships to the new item. Preserve correspondence serving its purpose and justify departures by requirements. Similar wording or appearance is insufficient.

For competing patterns, expose unresolved correspondence rather than assert a universal convention. Return the matched or proposed item, necessary differences, and reasons.

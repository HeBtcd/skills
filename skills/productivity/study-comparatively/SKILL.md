# Study Comparatively

Establish each subject's mechanism from evidence, then compare against shared questions and criteria. Align consequential conditions; bound conclusions where alignment fails.

Separate observed differences from causal explanations. Propose comparable experiments for consequential uncertainty without presenting them as collected evidence.

Return sources, comparison, tradeoffs, and uncertainty. Recommend only when requested and supported by the objective.

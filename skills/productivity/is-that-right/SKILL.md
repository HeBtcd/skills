# Is That Right?

Check the challenged judgment against its strongest supported objection and distinguishing evidence. Challenge alone does not imply error; counterexamples must satisfy the original claim's scope.

Explain retention or revision, separating changed factual beliefs from decisions changed by objectives or authorization. Keep missing evidence or intent unresolved.

Return hold, revise, or unresolved, its basis, and what would change it.

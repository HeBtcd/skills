# Says Who?

For a behavior-changing choice, find its governing rule, derivation, or authorized decision and check meaning and scope. Explicit delegation grants authority within scope; convention, measurement, or familiar defaults cannot supply user intent.

Without that basis, show a concrete difference between possible results and the missing rule or owner decision. Return the governing basis or unresolved choice and consequence.

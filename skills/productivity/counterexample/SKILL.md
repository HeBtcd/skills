# Counterexample

Find the smallest admissible case contradicting the claim's own meaning and scope. Show admissibility and contradiction. One legal execution refutes a universal execution claim even if that execution is not guaranteed.

Stop at one valid counterexample and return its evidence. Otherwise identify unsupported admissibility assumptions or report searched and unsearched scope; no counterexample found is not proof.

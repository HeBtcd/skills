# Reverse Engineering

Use static analysis to explain observed behavior and identify distinguishing observations. Follow relevant data/control flow as evidence demands; symbols or decompiled names alone do not establish behavior.

Account for consequential instance, loaded state, and observation effects. A missed observation excludes behavior only with demonstrated coverage.

Return supported explanations, alternatives, and proposed observations needed to settle them. For live-inspection proposals, include restoration of introduced changes and execution state. Bound conclusions by collected evidence.
